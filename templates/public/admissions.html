Admissions & Enrolment

Enrol in four simple steps

Whether you are a learner, a professional or a school, the enrolment process is designed to be quick and clear.

How to enrol

From enquiry to first class

1. Choose a course

Review the schools and programmes to find the course that matches your goals.

2. Confirm your intake

Contact us to check the next intake, schedule and current fees for your course.

3. Complete registration

Provide your details, choose your mode of study and complete registration.

4. Start learning

Attend classes, complete practical work and receive your certificate on completion.

Good to know

Entry requirements & fees

Simple entry requirements

Most short courses are open to beginners. Relevant requirements are listed on each programme and confirmed at enrolment.

Transparent fees

Course fees are confirmed at enrolment. Featured five-day certificate programmes are advertised at K750.

Certificates

Certificate programmes are awarded on successful completion of the course.

Two locations

Training is available at Liberty Mall, Lusaka and Mitengo, Ndola.
